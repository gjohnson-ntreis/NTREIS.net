NTREIS Taps Industry Veteran René Galicia for Executive Vice President and General Counsel

October 9, 2025  ·  4 min read

NTREIS adopts a bold approach to compliance and regulatory matters.

DALLAS, TX: NTREIS, the premier multiple listing service (MLS) in Texas and Louisiana, today announced the appointment of industry veteran and licensed attorney René Galicia as Executive Vice President and General Counsel. Galicia will oversee strategic operations and serve as NTREIS’s principal legal advisor, reinforcing the organization’s commitment to mitigating operational risk, regulatory leadership, and delivering value to subscribers amid a rapidly evolving real estate landscape.

René Galicia

EVP & General Counsel

Galicia brings over two decades of real estate industry experience, including nearly 15 years in progressive MLS leadership roles. Most recently, he served as Chief Operating Officer of the Council of Multiple Listing Services (CMLS), the national trade organization for more than 235 member MLSs. There, he oversaw daily operations, spearheaded member engagement, advanced thought leadership on key industry issues, and delivered internal legal support and strategic guidance.

A Newly Created Role

In this newly created role at NTREIS, Galicia will support strategic planning; member and shareholder engagement; technology and data governance; vendor and partnership management; compliance and risk management; and business development efforts. He joins NTREIS at a pivotal moment as the MLS advances modernization initiatives and strengthens its national voice while elevating local service.

“René brings a rare combination of deep MLS operational experience and legal expertise that is exactly what this moment demands. The industry is at a crossroads of policy, technology, and litigation, reshaping consumer expectations in real time. René’s track record at Bright MLS, CMLS, NAR, and CRMLS demonstrates his ability to build durable systems, modernize with purpose, and anticipate what’s next. His leadership will help us move decisively and deliver even greater value to our subscribers and shareholders.”Chris Carrillo, CEO, NTREIS
“NTREIS is making thoughtful, future-forward moves to serve our market and lead nationally. Creating this role and recruiting a leader of René’s caliber underscores our commitment to strong governance, compliance, and execution. As we realign and focus resources for the next chapter, the Board is fully supportive of this direction and confident that René’s experience will accelerate progress across our strategic initiatives.”Kristi Davis, President, NTREIS Board of Directors

A Track Record Across the Industry

Before CMLS, Galicia served as EVP, Policy Counsel & Strategic Partnerships at Bright MLS, leading customer operations for the largest MLS in the nation. He led customer satisfaction efforts, major efficiency gains, and expansion via acquisitions and partnerships. Previously, he served as Director of MLS Engagement at NAR, supporting 1.4 million members, and he held leadership roles at CRMLS, ultimately directing Compliance & Data Licensing for 38 associations.

“I am honored to join NTREIS at such a pivotal moment for the MLS industry. As a trusted partner for the Texas and Louisiana real estate community, NTREIS has built a strong foundation, and I look forward to collaborating with Chris, the executive team, and the Board of Directors. I aim to blend operational excellence with proactive legal strategies, positioning NTREIS as a forward-thinking leader amid evolving technology and diverse subscriber needs, while delivering exceptional value and service.”René Galicia, EVP & General Counsel, NTREIS

A licensed attorney (JD, Pacific Coast University School of Law), Galicia specializes in MLS legal and operational matters and holds CMLX3 (plus CMLX1 and CMLX2), RCE, Professional Standards Administrator, and RESO RED-B certifications. He has also served on the CMLS Board and NAR’s Federal Technology Policy Committee.

About NTREIS

NTREIS is the primary source of comprehensive real estate information and MLS services for Texas and Louisiana, serving 50,000+ subscribers across 44 counties. Through state-of-the-art technology and an unwavering commitment to innovation, NTREIS empowers real estate professionals with the data and tools they need to succeed.
