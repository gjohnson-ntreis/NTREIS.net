MLS Platforms

Core platforms for searching, listing, and managing client relationships.

MatrixThe primary NTREIS MLS: search, input, CMA, market reports, and prospect portals.
Included with your NTREIS subscription

Matrix is the primary NTREIS MLS system. Use it to search listings, enter and manage your own, run CMAs and market reports, and keep clients up to date through prospect portals and saved searches.

Matrix connects with Cotality’s other tools, including Realist Tax, so public records and listing data sit side by side. It works on desktop, tablet, and phone.

Log in to MatrixTraining and support
Paragon ConnectMobile-first MLS with add and edit, search, and client communications.
Included with your NTREIS subscription

Paragon Connect is an MLS platform from ICE that works the same on desktop, tablet, and phone. Search, add and edit listings, and stay in touch with clients from one place.

Its Collaboration Center gives you and your clients a shared portal to review listings and talk in real time, and EasyListing uses speech-to-text and error checks to speed up listing entry.

Log in to Paragon ConnectTraining and support
Cloud MLXModern, fast MLS search built for speed.
Included with your NTREIS subscription

CloudMLX is a simple, fast way to search the MLS. Start typing an address, area, or agent name and results appear as you type. Save searches, draw your own map areas, and gather homes into Collections to review with clients.

It works on any device and connects to Cloud CMA and Cloud Streams in one click.

Market Data & Analytics

Tools to understand the market and validate your data.

TaxProperty tax records for 254 Texas counties and 7 Louisiana parishes.
Included with your NTREIS subscription

Realist Tax gives you public records, tax history, ownership and mortgage details, and automated home value estimates for 254 Texas counties and 7 Louisiana parishes. Open it right from the MLS.

Use it to research any property, find likely sellers with farming tools and mailing labels, and create branded property reports for client meetings.

Log in to the MLSTraining and support
TrendsMarket trend reports for social, web, and newsletters.
Included with your NTREIS subscription

NTREIS Trends turns live MLS data into charts you can read in seconds. Compare up to four areas or draw your own on the map, then filter by price, property type, size, and more to see prices, inventory, days on market, and sales activity.

Share any chart as a PDF, a link, or a live widget on your website, with your contact info added automatically. New monthly data arrives on the 1st.

Transaction DeskDigital transaction management and document storage.
BrokerBayShowing scheduling built for agents and offices.
Showing management from Supra

BrokerBay is a cloud-based showing scheduler that pulls in your NTREIS listings automatically. Book, confirm, and reschedule showings, plan multi-property tours, and keep your office coordinated from your phone or desk.
