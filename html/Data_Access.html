Frequently Asked Questions.

View our FAQs for more information about licensing NTREIS data.

dataservices@ntreis.net
How do I apply for data, and what is the process?

All new data applications, including Broker Back Office (BBO) access, go through the Trestle RESO Web API. To apply, visit the Trestle Subscription Wizard, and see this article for step-by-step directions. You will work directly with Trestle to set up your feed, and fees are paid directly to Trestle. For help with setup or billing, email trestlesupport@cotality.com. For questions about NTREIS data access, email dataservices@ntreis.net.

What is the approval process?

Our team reviews the application, intended use case, and applicable compliance requirements. Once approved, you’ll need to sign the licensing agreement and set up your feed directly with Trestle; after that, your credentials will be issued.

What are common reasons NTREIS licenses data?

Common use cases include Consumer Display & Delivery (IDX/VOW), analytics, marketing, use in back-office systems such as CMA, CRM, or production systems, and licensing brokers their own data back. Two of the most frequent requests are outlined below.

Consumer Display Websites (IDX/VOW)

For brokers and agents who want to display active listing data for consumers to search on their own website, or for the vendors building and hosting that site on their behalf.

  • Multi-language IDX: available through Immobel. Click here for more information.
  • Working with a vendor: see our list of authorized vendors. Vendors charge their own fees for the services they provide. If your vendor isn’t on the list, they can apply to become an authorized NTREIS vendor through the Trestle Subscription Wizard.
  • Matrix users: can activate the built-in Agent Web Page under “My Matrix,” which includes an IDX search at no additional cost. Click here for more information.
Back-Office Access

For MLS Participants who need NTREIS data for internal, in-office use only, for example, back-office systems that produce CMAs, CRMs, or production statistics.

Broker Back Office (BBO) access is available through the Trestle RESO Web API. Apply through the Trestle Subscription Wizard, describing your intended use of the MLS data in detail. Once approved, the Participant will complete a data access agreement before credentials are issued. Contact Trestle directly to set up your feed at trestlesupport@cotality.com. Questions about NTREIS data access? Email dataservices@ntreis.net.

Who is data typically licensed to?
  • Vendors: companies that provide the product or service a broker or agent uses.
  • Brokers: licensing their own data back, or approving NTREIS to send their data to a vendor they work with.
  • Agents: seeking an IDX or VOW feed.
What are the compliance requirements?

Compliance requirements center primarily on consumer display. If you operate a website displaying NTREIS data, it must include broker attribution (listing agent and brokerage firm name), contact information, the last-updated date/time, an NTREIS disclaimer, and identification of the data source.

Applications that include AI-powered functionality may require additional review to ensure compliance with NTREIS licensing requirements.

Websites submitted for approval must be accessible for review. If a site is still under development or requires login credentials, please provide access instructions or a demonstration environment so NTREIS can complete its compliance review.

Approved IDX and VOW websites are expected to be live and fully compliant within two weeks of approval. NTREIS also performs periodic compliance reviews of approved websites; failure to maintain compliance may result in suspension of the data feed.

What is the pricing?

Vendors and brokers set up their data feed directly with Trestle and pay Trestle directly. For pricing and billing questions, email trestlesupport@cotality.com. To see a list of currently approved vendors, click here.

3. Broker Back Office (BBO)

BBO access for in-house office use

MLS Participants may request access to NTREIS MLS data for their in-house office use only. This is typically needed for a back-office software program or application that produces CMAs, statistics, and similar reports for the broker. BBO access is available through the Trestle RESO Web API.

Apply through Trestle. For step-by-step directions, see our Trestle Web API article. When you apply, describe how the MLS data will be used. You will contact Trestle directly to set up your feed and pay Trestle directly for it. For help with setup or billing, email trestlesupport@cotality.com.
