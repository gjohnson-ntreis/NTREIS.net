NTREIS Email Signatures

Pick your name, copy your signature, and follow the steps for your version of Outlook below. Every signature uses the same approved layout, so please don't edit the fonts or colors after you paste.

Which Outlook do you have?

  • Mac: you're on a MacBook or iMac.
  • Classic Outlook (Windows): there's a File tab in the top-left corner of Outlook.
  • New Outlook (Windows) or Outlook on the web: there's no File tab, and a gear icon sits in the top-right corner.

Mac

  1. Pick your name above and click Copy signature.
  2. Open Outlook. In the menu bar at the very top of your screen, click Outlook, then Settings.
  3. Click Signatures.
  4. Click the + button under the list to make a new signature. Name it NTREIS.
  5. Click inside the big empty box and press Cmd+V. Your signature appears with the logo and icons.
  6. Under Choose default signature, set New messages and Replies/forwards to NTREIS.
  7. Click Save, or close the window.

Classic Outlook (Windows)

  1. Pick your name above and click Copy signature.
  2. In Outlook, click File in the top-left corner, then Options at the bottom of the left column.
  3. Click Mail in the left column, then the Signatures... button on the right.
  4. Click New, type NTREIS, and click OK.
  5. Click inside the big box at the bottom and press Ctrl+V.
  6. Look on the right side of the window. Set New messages to NTREIS and Replies/forwards to NTREIS.
  7. Click OK, then OK again.

New Outlook (Windows) or Outlook on the web

  1. Pick your name above and click Copy signature.
  2. In Outlook, click the gear icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Click Account, then Signatures.
  4. Click + New signature and name it NTREIS.
  5. Click inside the big box and press Ctrl+V.
  6. Click Save.
  7. Below that, under Select default signatures, set both For new messages and For replies/forwards to NTREIS, then click Save.
Paste not working in classic Outlook? Use the download instead
  1. Pick your name above and click Download for classic Outlook. Close Outlook.
  2. Open File Explorer (the yellow folder icon on your taskbar).
  3. Click the address bar at the top, type %APPDATA%\Microsoft\Signatures, and press Enter. This is the folder where Outlook keeps signatures.
  4. Open your Downloads folder in a second window and double-click the zip that starts with NTREIS-Signature.
  5. Select everything inside the zip (Ctrl+A) and drag it into the Signatures folder.
  6. Open Outlook and do steps 2, 3, 6 and 7 of the classic steps above, picking NTREIS on the right side.
