NTREIS Email Signatures

Pick your name, copy your signature, and paste it into Outlook. Every signature uses the same approved layout, so please don't edit the fonts or colors after you paste.

Outlook on Mac

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Outlook, open Settings, then Signatures.
  3. Open your signature, press Cmd+A, then Delete.
  4. Press Cmd+V and Save.

Outlook on Windows

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Outlook, go to File, Options, Mail, then Signatures.
  3. Select your signature, press Ctrl+A, then Delete.
  4. Press Ctrl+V and click OK.

Send yourself a test email after you paste. Something wrong, or your details changed? Email Sheree Tomba at sheree@ntreis.net and the page will be updated.
