About NTREIS.

Short version

NTREIS is the premier MLS in Texas and Louisiana, serving 50,000+ subscribers across 44 counties.

Long version

NTREIS is the premier MLS in Texas and Louisiana and the primary source of comprehensive real estate information and MLS services for the region. Founded in 1998 and owned by 13 member REALTOR® associations, NTREIS serves 50,000+ subscribers across 44 counties with the data, tools, and standards that keep the regional real estate market accurate, transparent, and efficient. Through state-of-the-art technology and an unwavering commitment to its subscribers, NTREIS continues to modernize the MLS experience for brokers, agents, and the consumers they serve.

Leadership

Executive bios.

Chris Carrillo

Chief Executive Officer

Chris Carrillo became Chief Executive Officer of NTREIS in June 2025, bringing more than two decades of multiple listing service leadership to the role. His career began in a technology support role at Chicago's MLS and grew into executive leadership across the country: he was named CEO of an Akron, Ohio area MLS at age 29, then spent 14 years leading Metro MLS in Milwaukee before joining NTREIS.

A U.S. Army veteran who served in military police, Carrillo has prioritized technology investment at NTREIS, launched a broker rewards program that compensates brokers for the content they contribute to the MLS, and is leading an initiative to centralize compliance across NTREIS's 13 member associations. He holds a degree from Northern Illinois University.

René Galicia

Executive Vice President & Chief Legal Counsel

René Galicia serves as Executive Vice President and Chief Legal Counsel of NTREIS, a newly created role he stepped into in October 2025. A licensed attorney with a JD from Pacific Coast University School of Law, Galicia brings more than two decades of real estate industry experience and nearly 15 years in progressive MLS leadership roles, most recently as Chief Operating Officer of the Council of Multiple Listing Services (CMLS), the national trade organization for more than 235 member MLSs.

Earlier in his career, Galicia served as EVP, Policy Counsel & Strategic Partnerships at Bright MLS, the nation's largest MLS; as Director of MLS Engagement at the National Association of REALTORS®, supporting 1.4 million members; and directed Compliance & Data Licensing for 38 associations at CRMLS. He holds CMLX3, CMLX1, and CMLX2 certifications, along with RCE, Professional Standards Administrator, and RESO RED-B credentials.
