NTREIS Names Mike Murphy as Senior Vice President of Integrated Solutions

May 29, 2026  ·  4 min read

New role reinforces NTREIS’s commitment to operational excellence, modernization, and a scalable, partner-driven future.

DALLAS, TX, May 29, 2026: NTREIS, the premier multiple listing service (MLS) in Texas and Louisiana, today announced the appointment of Mike Murphy as Senior Vice President, Integrated Solutions. This newly created role reflects NTREIS’s investment in modernization, strategic partnerships, and delivering an integrated, high-performance MLS ecosystem for its subscribers and stakeholders.

Mike Murphy

SVP, Integrated Solutions

Murphy joins NTREIS with more than two decades of MLS-specific experience spanning operations, technology, and vendor integration. His appointment comes at a pivotal time as NTREIS works to ensure the organization remains nimble, future-ready, and equipped to respond to ongoing industry change and innovation.

Advancing Integrated Solutions and Operational Excellence

As Senior Vice President, Integrated Solutions, Murphy will serve as a member of the senior leadership team, contributing to organizational strategy and cross-functional decision-making while leading NTREIS’s partner integrations and data services initiatives. His role will focus on aligning technology, vendor partnerships, and internal operations to deliver seamless, high-value solutions that support the organization’s commitment to excellence.

Murphy will oversee the full lifecycle of third-party technology partnerships, including evaluation, onboarding, performance management, and long-term strategy. He will also define and lead the product roadmap for integration offerings, ensuring alignment with subscriber needs, organizational priorities, and evolving industry standards.

This role directly supports NTREIS’s strategic goal to provide industry-leading operational excellence through the systemic optimization of processes, technology, and people, delivering faster resolution times, higher customer satisfaction, and embedding a strong, consistent customer service culture across all departments.

Industry Expertise and Proven Leadership

Mike Murphy is a respected leader in the real estate technology industry with more than two decades of experience in MLS operations, technology, and strategic leadership. Formerly serving as Chief Operations Officer for Central Texas MLS (CTXMLS), Mike has built a career centered on innovation, collaboration, and advancing the real estate industry through technology and operational excellence. His prior experience includes roles at CoreLogic as a Project Manager in the Matrix division and at Rapattoni Corporation, where he progressed from Applications Programmer to MLS Staff Support Manager, working closely with MLS organizations nationwide.

“Mike brings a rare combination of deep operational expertise and technical acumen that is exactly what NTREIS needs at this moment. He’s going to bring his decades of MLS operational and technical skills to NTREIS at a perfect time. As we look to onboard compliance and modernize our MLS platforms, his experience will provide needed horsepower and perspective to the team.”Chris Carrillo, CEO, NTREIS
“I am honored to join NTREIS at a defining moment for the organization and the MLS industry. Chris and Renée have a clear vision for modernization and growth, and I look forward to helping build integrated solutions that position NTREIS to lead through this next phase while delivering meaningful value to subscribers.”Mike Murphy, SVP, Integrated Solutions, NTREIS

Currently, Mike serves as the 2026 Chair of the Texas REALTORS® MLS & Technology Committee and holds both the CMLX3 and C2EX designations. Throughout his career, he has participated in and hosted industry panels at both state and national levels, contributing thought leadership and practical insight to conversations shaping the future of real estate technology.

A Strategic Step Forward

Murphy’s appointment marks another key step in NTREIS’s broader effort to modernize its infrastructure, strengthen its partner ecosystem, and scale its operations in alignment with industry evolution. His leadership is expected to accelerate progress across integration strategy, vendor management, and service delivery while reinforcing NTREIS’s position as a forward-thinking leader in the MLS space.

About NTREIS

NTREIS is the primary source of comprehensive real estate information and MLS services for Texas and Louisiana, serving 50,000+ subscribers across 44 counties. Through state-of-the-art technology and an unwavering commitment to innovation, NTREIS empowers real estate professionals with the data and tools they need to succeed.
