About the program

The data that drives real estate starts with brokers.

Brokers drive the marketplace. The listings they input fuel transparency for buyers and sellers, power fair housing, and give the entire industry something to build on. As NTREIS CEO Chris Carrillo puts it: "Broker participants should be the focal point of everything we do going forward, and this program is a tangible expression of that commitment."

Every listing that populates real estate portals, powers market reports, and drives consumer search began with a broker's work. NTREIS Rewards is built on a straightforward principle: when brokers contribute their data to the MLS, that contribution should come with a return.

For the 2025 program year, NTREIS is distributing a seven-figure total program investment to qualifying brokers, self-funded from NTREIS operating reserves with no outside deals and no conditions attached. This is not a gesture. It is a commitment.

How much will I receive?

Your reward amount depends on your listing volume and the quality of your contributions: factors like timely listings entered, completeness and richness of content such as photos and virtual tours, and whether listings progressed successfully to closed. Every qualifying listing is evaluated on the same criteria regardless of brokerage size.

Is this taxable?

As part of the registration process you will submit standard tax documentation. Consult your tax advisor for guidance specific to your situation.

Will this happen again?

Yes. This is the inaugural year of NTREIS Rewards. Beginning with the 2027 distribution, the program shifts to being funded by data licensing revenue, so it grows as the value of broker data grows.

Eligibility

How to qualify

01

Minimum 10 Listings

You must have entered at least 10 listings into the NTREIS MLS during the 2025 calendar year. The threshold is deliberately achievable for solo and small-team brokers, not just large operations.

02

Quality, Timeliness and Completeness

Listings are evaluated on a points-based system that weighs content richness, including photos and virtual tours, alongside other objective MLS activity. The program rewards quality, not just volume.

03

Successful Progression

Listings that moved successfully through to sold and closed are part of the evaluation, rewarding brokers whose listings represent the full arc of a real estate transaction.

What happens next

Instructions are on the way.

Qualifying brokers will receive an email with everything they need to complete registration and receive their payment electronically. Look for emails coming from ntreis.net starting in July 2026.
